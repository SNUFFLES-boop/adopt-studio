Avatar Switcher

Control VRChat on your PC from your phone.

Where to find the address

  1. On your PC, open Avatar Switcher and go to Settings: OSC and phone access.
  2. Tick Phone access and save.
  3. It shows "On your phone, open http://192.168.x.x:9010". Type that address here.
  4. Then enter the 6-digit PIN it shows.

Your phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi.
