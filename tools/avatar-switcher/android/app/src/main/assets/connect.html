Avatar Switcher

Control VRChat on your PC from your phone.

Where to find the address

  1. On your PC, open Avatar Switcher.
  2. At the top, in 📱 Connect your phone, click Turn on phone access.
  3. It shows an address like 192.168.1.20. Type it here and tap Connect.
  4. Then enter the 6-digit PIN it shows.

Your phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi.
